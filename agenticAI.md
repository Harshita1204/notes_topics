TOPIC: AGENTIC AI


1. Definition
------------------------------------------------------------
Agentic AI refers to AI systems that can act
autonomously to achieve a specific goal.

Unlike simple AI that only responds to input,
agentic AI can plan, decide, and take actions
on its own.


2. Key Characteristics
------------------------------------------------------------
- Goal-oriented behavior.
- Makes decisions independently.
- Can plan multiple steps ahead.
- Adjusts actions based on feedback.


3. How It Works (Conceptually)
------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Receives a goal.
Step 2: Breaks it into smaller tasks.
Step 3: Executes actions.
Step 4: Observes results and adjusts.

It operates like an intelligent agent.


4. Example
------------------------------------------------------------
- An AI assistant that books tickets automatically.
- AI that manages inventory and places orders.
- Autonomous driving systems.

It does not just answer — it performs actions.


5. Difference from Generative AI
------------------------------------------------------------
Generative AI:
- Creates content (text, image, code).

Agentic AI:
- Takes actions to complete tasks.
- Can use tools and external systems.


6. Importance
------------------------------------------------------------
- Used in automation systems.
- Improves productivity.
- Enables intelligent decision-making.
- Supports advanced AI applications.
